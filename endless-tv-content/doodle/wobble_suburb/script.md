# Wendell in: One Lousy Dandelion

*A Doodle cartoon short in the wobble_suburb style: hand-inked boiling-line cels over gouache-painted suburbia, 1990s TV slapstick.*

**Logline:** Dad's lawn has to be perfect by the time he gets home, and there's one lousy dandelion in it. Ten-year-old Wendell tries his hands, a spade and Mom's vacuum. Each attempt makes the yard worse, until his victory seeds the whole lawn.

**Runtime:** about 3.0 s title card, 11 shots (2724 frames = 113.5 s), 2.0 s end card, for about 118.5 s (1:58.5) in total.

## Characters (fixed descriptions)

- **Wendell Pruitt (10)**: Wendell, a lanky ten-year-old boy with a scruffy tangerine mop of hair, a long pickle-shaped nose, two big buck teeth, freckles, a sky-blue polo shirt with a white collar, knee-length khaki shorts, tall white socks with red bands and huge red sneakers. Voice: *a ten-year-old boy, nasal, scratchy and over-excited, full of cartoon bravado*.
- **Bea Pruitt (6)**: Bea, his little sister, a small round-headed six-year-old girl with two black curly hair puffs tied with yellow bobbles, sleepy half-closed eyes, a yellow T-shirt under purple overalls and pink sneakers, sitting in a green-and-white webbed aluminium lawn chair. Voice: *a six-year-old girl, flat, dry and slow, completely deadpan*.
- **Dad**: never seen, in the tradition of the genre. Only his car horn is heard.
- **The Dandelion**: one shaggy yellow bloom on a jagged-leaf rosette, utterly unbothered.
